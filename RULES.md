## 1. Base rules

**1.1** The ITTF rules for doubles table tennis apply. [1] If an ITTF rule conflicts with a rule in this document, this document applies.

## 2. Lobs

**2.1** Each player may strike the ball once per return. The exception is the first return of the service. On that return, only the receiver may strike the ball.

**2.2** The first player to strike the ball on a return is the **setter**. The setter's partner, when the return ends, is the **spiker**.

- **2.2.1** The setter may return the ball directly. Or the setter may strike the ball so the spiker can return it to the opponents' side of the table.
- **2.2.2** When both the setter and the spiker strike the ball on a return, the return is a **lob**.

**2.3** During a lob, one hand of the spiker may touch any part of the table.

**2.4** Lob scoring:

- **2.4.1** A lob that scores directly after a one-hit return is worth **2 points**.
- **2.4.2** A lob that scores after a lob by the opponents is worth **N + 1 points**. N is the number of consecutive lobs by both teams since the last one-hit return.

  *Example:* The serving team lobs at the first legal chance. The receiving team lobs back and scores. There are 2 consecutive lobs, so the scoring lob is worth **3 points**.

## 3. Service

**3.1** If a valid service strikes the edge of the table, the server's partner may strike the ball.

- **3.1.1** If the partner strikes the ball, the rally counts as a lob.
- **3.1.2** If the partner does not strike the ball, the opposing team scores a point.

## 4. Returns and play

**4.1** Players on the returning team may strike the ball in any order.

**4.2** If the opponents return a lob with a one-hit return, the team that hit the lob must alternate hits again. The setter of that lob strikes first.

**4.3** The ball may be played after it strikes any surface or object beyond the playing table, except the floor. Examples include walls, ceilings, support pillars, and spectators.

## 5. Offside

**5.1** The offside line is the back edge of the table. It is parallel to the net and extends out from the table indefinitely.

**5.2** When the spiker strikes the ball, the spiker's entire lower body must be behind the offside line.

- **5.2.1** The lower body is the part of the body below the height of the playing table.
- **5.2.2** The spiker may lean over the offside line with the upper body.

## 6. Scoring the game

**6.1** A game is played to 21 points. A match is best of 3 games.

**6.2** The winning team must lead by at least 2 points.

**6.3** The winning point must be a lob. The exception is the Three-Strike rule in 6.4.

**6.4** Three-Strike (Filippini) rule

- **6.4.1** If a team is within one point of a winning score and scores a point that is not a lob, the opponents receive a strike.
- **6.4.2** A team that wins at least one point has its strikes reset to zero.
- **6.4.3** A team with 3 strikes loses the game.
- **6.4.4** When any team reaches game point, all team members must say aloud: "The Three-Strike (Filippini) rule is in effect."
